Reel Finder Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Reel Finder is a browser extension that finds where an Instagram reel sits in its creator's profile grid. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension accesses

  • Instagram pages you open. The extension runs only on www.instagram.com. It reads the profile grid (post links and thumbnails) and the post data Instagram's own page has already loaded, so it can show captions and dates.
  • The author of a reel. When you click "Find in profile", it makes one request to Instagram, using your existing Instagram login, to find out who posted the reel.
  • A link you paste into the popup. The extension stores it briefly in your browser's local extension storage so the Instagram tab can pick it up, then deletes it.

What it does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or any third party. The extension has no server.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies of its own.
  • It does not read your messages, passwords or account settings, and it does not post, like, follow or comment.
  • It does not keep a history of the reels you search for.

Data storage

Everything happens inside your browser. The only thing saved is a pasted link, and it is removed as soon as it is used. Uninstalling the extension removes that storage.

Not affiliated with Instagram
